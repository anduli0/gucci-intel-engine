GMAI는 구찌를 향한 시장 온도를 0~100으로 잰 체온계다 — 50이 보통, 높을수록 우호적이다. 재료는 넷이다. 감성(좋게 말하는가), 화제(뉴스·SNS·커뮤니티를 합쳐 얼마나 많이 이야기되는가, 건수만), 참여(한 건당 반응이 얼마나 뜨거운가, 좋아요·공유만), 점유(경쟁자 대비 몫). 화제와 참여는 절대 섞이지 않는다 — 하나는 '양', 다른 하나는 '강도'다.

오늘 올라간 점수는 민심 개선이 아니라 화제량이 끌어올린 반등으로 읽어야 한다 — 변화가 가장 큰 북미(Δ+7.14)와 유럽(Δ+6.14)의 감성 기여가 각각 +4.22, +6.75로 컸지만, 이는 감성값이 높아진 게 아니라 전일의 깊은 바닥 대비 되돌림이고, 네 권역 감성은 여전히 0.39~0.55로 중립(0.5) 안팎이다[1]. 다만 균형추가 필요하다 — 분류된 민심은 부정이 더 많다. 북미는 11건 중 부정 6·긍정 0, 유럽은 16건 중 부정 6·긍정 2였고, 뿌리는 머라이어 캐리 홀리데이 캠페인에 쏠린 "차갑고 지루하다"는 조직적 반발[2][3], 유럽 품질·AS 불만[4], 그리고 3분기 실적(비교매출 -6%, 직영 -9%)의 잔향이다[5][8]. 본 분석의 판단은, 오늘의 +4는 '나아졌다'가 아니라 '어제 과하게 빠진 자리로 돌아왔다'가 정확한 묘사라는 것이다. 내일 볼 것은 셋이다. 먼저 화제량이 식으면 글로벌이 다시 36~38점대로 내려앉는지, 또 동남아 표본(6건·유효 3.5)이 두꺼워지는지, 마지막으로 어느 권역이든 감성값이 0.5를 넘는 날이 오는지다 — 그래야 '민심 회복'이라 부를 수 있다.

# The Number Went Up, Sentiment Didn't — GMAI 40.01, Δ+4.03, a Buzz-Driven Rebound While Sentiment Stays Weak in All Four Regions

## Today's Verdict

Gucci's report card rose 4 points from yesterday, but not because people are speaking better of the brand. The global GMAI is 40.01 out of 100, still below the 50-point midpoint, in the 주의/caution band. The best region is Northeast Asia (46.51), the worst Europe (32.23). The one thing worth watching today is that the force pushing the score up was not "are people saying better things" but "how much is being said."

## How to Read the Index

GMAI is a thermometer for market temperature toward Gucci, scored 0-100 — 50 is normal, higher is more favorable. It blends four ingredients: sentiment (whether people speak well of Gucci), buzz (how much Gucci is talked about across news, social media and forums combined, counting volume only), engagement (how hot the reaction is per post — likes and shares only), and SOV (share of voice versus competitors). Buzz and engagement are never mixed — one is quantity, the other is intensity.

## 1. Today's Numbers

The core finding is that today's global 40.01 (Δ+4.03) is explained almost entirely by buzz volume and a modest sentiment bounce-back — the delta attribution splits into sentiment +2.00, buzz +2.03, engagement -0.03 and SOV +0.02, meaning the first two ingredients account for 99% of the change[1].

## 2. Why It Moved, and What to Watch

Today's gain should be read as a buzz-driven rebound, not an improvement in opinion — North America (Δ+7.14) and Europe (Δ+6.14), the two biggest movers, had large sentiment contributions of +4.22 and +6.75 respectively, but this is not sentiment getting better, it is a bounce off yesterday's unusually deep trough; all four regions' sentiment values remain 0.39-0.55, around the 0.5 neutral mark[1]. A counterweight is needed, however — classified opinion still skews negative. North America logged 6 negative items against 0 positive out of 11, Europe 6 negative against 2 positive out of 16, and the root causes are organized backlash concentrated on the Mariah Carey holiday campaign being called "cold and boring"[2][3], EU quality/service complaints[4], and the lingering echo of Q3 earnings (comparable sales -6%, directly-operated retail -9%)[5][8]. Our read is that today's +4 is accurately described not as "improvement" but as "a return to a level from which it had fallen too far yesterday." Three things to watch tomorrow: whether the global score sinks back to the 36-38 range once buzz cools, whether SEA's sample (6 items, effective 3.5) thickens, and whether any region's sentiment value finally crosses 0.5 — only then would "a recovery in opinion" be the right description.

## Notes

- GMAI (Gucci Market Attractiveness Index): a 0-100 index of market favorability toward Gucci. 50 is normal, higher is more favorable. Today's 40.01 is below midpoint, in the "caution" range.
- Δ (delta, change): the change versus the prior calculation day (11-08). Today's global +4.03 means a 4-point rise in one day. Note this is measured against the "prior calculation day," not strictly "yesterday," since gap days can exaggerate the change.
- Band: the named score ranges, worsening in order Favorable > Neutral > Caution > Alert. "Neutral" sits around normal, "Caution" below it.
- NSS (net sentiment score): sentiment summarized on a -1 to +1 scale. Today's four regions range -0.23 to +0.11; only SEA is barely positive, the rest negative.
- Sentiment component: how favorably people speak of Gucci. 0-1, 0.5 is neutral. Today's 0.39-0.55 sits around neutral in all four regions.
- Buzz component: how much Gucci is talked about across news, social media and forums combined (count and influence-weighted). 0-1. NEA's 0.98 is very active, EU's 0.15 is very quiet. A "quantity" measure never mixed with engagement.
- Engagement component: reaction intensity per post (likes, shares, etc.). 0-1, 0.5 is platform average. Today is a measured value (v2.3, effective sample 48-125 items); 0.48-0.55 is average level.
- SOV component: Gucci's share of conversation versus competing houses. Since the 10-31 competitive-set expansion (8 to 18 brands) moved the neutral coordinate to 1/19, do not compare directly against historical figures — read against the new baseline. Today's range is 0.08-0.11.
- Contrib / delta_attrib: how many points each ingredient added to the score or to the change. Today, 99% of the global change came from sentiment (+2.00) and buzz (+2.03).
- eff_n (effective sample): the real thickness of the sample. SEA's 3.5 is very thin, a zone where daily swings are exaggerated; NEA's 10.7 is comparatively stable.
