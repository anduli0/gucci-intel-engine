## How to Read the Index

GMAI is a 0-100 report card for how attractive Gucci looks in the market right now — a thermometer where 50 is normal and higher is better. It blends four ingredients. "Sentiment" is whether people speak well of Gucci; "buzz" is how much Gucci is talked about across news, social media and forums combined (counting volume only); "engagement" is how hot the reaction is per post (likes, shares, and other reported metrics only); and "SOV" (share of voice) is how much of the conversation Gucci holds versus competing brands. Buzz and engagement are never mixed — one is quantity, the other is intensity.

Engagement values (0.47-0.53) across all four regions are merely "platform-average," not a sign of improvement, and the low 0.08-0.11 SOV values are a structural result of the wider competitive set, not a sudden drop.

- 점유(SOV) 성분: 0~1, 경쟁 브랜드 대비 구찌가 차지하는 이야기 비중. 모두 객관 집계. 0.08~0.11로 낮은데, 이는 경쟁군이 18개 브랜드로 넓어져 중립값 자체가 내려간 구조적 결과다.
- contrib(기여): 각 재료가 그 권역 GMAI 총점에 몇 점을 보탰는지. 예로 동남아 감성 22.98점은 그 권역 점수의 절반 이상을 감성이 떠받친다는 뜻.
- delta_attrib(변화 분해): Δ를 재료별로 쪼갠 값. 북미 Δ-15.97은 감성 -7.96 + 화제 -7.55로 거의 반반.
- 표본·유효표본(eff_n): 그날 분류된 게시물 수와 가중 후 실질 표본. 동남아 8건(유효 7.1)은 작은 변화도 지수를 크게 흔드는 저표본 구간이다.

## Notes

- GMAI: a composite 0-100 report card for how attractive Gucci looks in the market. 50 is normal, higher is better. Today's global 35.98 is well below midpoint, in the "caution" range.
- Band: the four named ranges of GMAI, worsening in order Favorable > Neutral > Caution > Alert. Only Europe is in the lowest "alert" band today (26.09); the rest are Caution to Neutral.
- Δ (delta, change): the GMAI change versus the prior calculation day (Nov 5). Today's global -7.21 means more than 7 points were lost in one day.
- NSS: net sentiment score, -1 to +1, 0 is neutral. Europe's -0.42 and North America's -0.40 mean the tilt is negative.
- Sentiment component: 0-1, how favorably people speak of Gucci; 0.5 is even. NA's 0.30 and EU's 0.29 show negative dominance.
- Buzz component: 0-1, volume of news/social/forum mentions scaled against history (quantity, not intensity). Europe's 0.17 means buzz is well below its usual level. The 8-day baseline has accumulated, so the fixed 0.5 placeholder period has passed.
- Engagement component: 0-1, heat of reaction per post (likes, shares, etc.). All four regions carry measured values today (v2.3); 0.47-0.53 is "platform-average." Based on YouTube, Bluesky, Mastodon and Reddit reaction data.
- SOV component: 0-1, Gucci's share of conversation versus competing brands. Fully measured. The low 0.08-0.11 range reflects the competitive set widening to 18 brands, which structurally lowers the neutral baseline.
- Contrib: how many points each ingredient added to that region's GMAI total. For example, SEA's sentiment contribution of 22.98 points means sentiment alone carries more than half of that region's score.
- Delta_attrib: the Δ broken down by ingredient. NA's Δ-15.97 splits almost evenly into sentiment -7.96 and buzz -7.55.
- Sample / effective sample (eff_n): the count of posts classified that day, and the weighted effective count. SEA's 8 items (effective 7.1) is a low-sample zone where small shifts swing the index heavily.
- §3.5 trigger: an automatic anomaly alert. Four fired today (global |Δ|>=7, NA and EU drops >=10pt, EU in alert band) — flagged for event-response review.
